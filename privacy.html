Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy covers every Android app published by Sapp Labs:

PDF Reader — com.gappstudios.autowifi3gdataswitch.san.basicpdfviewer
Docx Reader — com.docxreader.sapplabs.docxreader
Excel Reader — com.sapplabs.excelreader

We're a small, independent developer, not a company with a data-collection business model. These apps are built to do one job — open a file — and get out of your way.

Your files

Every file you open is read and rendered entirely on your device. None of these apps upload, transmit, or otherwise send the contents of your files anywhere. Files are only ever read — never modified or deleted by the app itself.

The "All files access" permission

Each app requests Android's All files access permission (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) so it can list and open supported files already on your device, without you having to pick each one individually through a file browser. This permission is used solely to find and open files of the type that app supports (PDF, DOCX, or XLSX/CSV) — it is never used to read, modify, or transmit any other file or data on your device. You can decline this permission and still open individual files directly through your device's file manager or another app's "Share"/"Open with" menu.

Advertising (PDF Reader and Docx Reader only)

PDF Reader and Docx Reader are supported by ads so they can stay free. They use Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) as an ad mediation service, which may serve ads from these networks: Unity Ads, ironSource Ads, Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network. Excel Reader does not currently include any ads, ad SDKs, or internet access.

To serve and measure ads, these mediation and ad-network SDKs may collect:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • General device and app information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version)
  • Approximate ad interaction data (e.g. which ads were shown or tapped)
  • IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location may be inferred. The apps never ask for or use your device's precise GPS location.
  • A list of other apps installed on your device, used by our ad mediation provider for ad relevance and fraud prevention

This data may be shared with Unity, Google, Meta, and the specific ad networks active in a given ad request, for the purpose of serving and measuring ads and preventing ad fraud. Each of these companies handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity's Privacy Policy
  • Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Meta's Privacy Policy

Some of these companies may process this data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies.

Your privacy choices

PDF Reader and Docx Reader use Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform, to collect and remember your ad privacy choices.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the app shows a consent form. You choose whether the ad partners may use your data for personalized ads and the other purposes listed in the form. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized ads, which are based on things like the app you're using rather than your profile. Your choice is stored on your device in the standard IAB Transparency & Consent Framework format and passed to our ad partners. Where data is processed for personalized ads, the legal basis is your consent; some processing, such as ad fraud prevention and security, may rely on the ad partners' legitimate interests, which you can object to in the same form.
  • United States: if you live in a state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut or Utah), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Changing your choice: open the app's menu (⋮) and tap Privacy settings at any time to review, change or withdraw your choice. This item appears wherever the law gives you that right.
  • On any Android device: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device), which stops it being used to personalize ads.

Excel Reader has no ads, so it collects nothing and has no choices to make here.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Sapp Labs itself holds no personal data about you: we have no accounts, servers or analytics. Data collected by the ad partners is held by them, so requests about it are best sent to them through the policies linked above. You can always contact us and we'll help, and you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Ratings

After you've opened a few files, an app may show Google Play's own rating card. It is run entirely by the Google Play Store app; we never see who rated or what they wrote unless it's published on Google Play.

What we don't collect

None of these apps ask for your name, email address, phone number, or postal address, and none of them require creating an account or signing in.

Children's privacy

These apps are general-audience utility apps and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and none of these apps participate in Google Play's Designed for Families program.

Data retention and deletion

Since there's no account or server-side profile, uninstalling an app removes all of its local data from your device. Any data collected by the third-party ad SDKs described above is retained according to those companies' own policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date above.
